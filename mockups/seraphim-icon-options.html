SERAPHIM STUDIO — ICON OPTIONS

9 directions · pick favourites · pair with the cyberpunk skin · drop into src-tauri/icons/

Each icon below is built with pure CSS + inline SVG so we can iterate fast. To ship one as the real app icon, we render the chosen design at 1024×1024 PNG, run it through tauri icon to generate the platform sizes (32×32, 128×128, 128×128@2x, .icns for Mac, .ico for Windows), and replace the files in src-tauri/icons/. Bundle identifier stays the same so existing installs auto-update without losing prefs.
01 · NEON HALO + WINGS
Refined version of the current icon. White wings get a cyan glow, halo gold becomes the alarm-yellow accent. Closest to "evolution not revolution" — existing users won't be surprised.
PRO: continuity · CON: similar to existing
02 · GEOMETRIC SERAPH
Six-winged seraph as a hexagram + two flanking triangles. Looks like a sigil. Good for "this is a serious tool" branding without being literal.
PRO: distinctive · sigil-like · CON: abstract
SS
03 · MONOGRAM SS
Stacked Audiowide "SS" in alarm yellow on a pink-to-black gradient. Reads at 32×32 better than any other option here. Easiest to recognize on a crowded dock or taskbar.
PRO: readable @ 32px · CON: literal/typographic
04 · CIRCUIT-WING
Angular wings with PCB-trace detail in cyan + pink. Most "cyberpunk" of the bunch — wings are clearly tech, not heavenly. Pairs cleanly with the in-app cyberpunk skin.
PRO: strong skin match · CON: less divine
S
05 · HOLOGRAPHIC HALO
Rotating iridescent ring around an "S" core. The ring animates 8s — would be still on the actual icon (animation is just for preview here). Reads as both halo and aurora.
PRO: premium feel · CON: only ring renders well at 32px
06 · CYBERPUNK BADGE
Clip-path corner cuts (top-left + bottom-right) match the in-app logo + button shape exactly. Single yellow "S" with neon double-shadow. Most consistent with skin.
PRO: identical to in-app shape · CON: literal
07 · PIXEL HALO
8-bit / arcade pixel-art seraph. Halo + central body + outstretched wings using only square pixels. Stands out hard from every other tool on the dock — but commits to the joke.
PRO: memorable · CON: niche aesthetic
08 · DIVINE CYBERPUNK
Yellow halo + clip-path wings in white-to-pink gradient with neon glow. Combines literal angel iconography with cyberpunk material treatment. My pick — best of both.
PRO: balances both worlds · MID
09 · SACRED GEOMETRY
Halo + nested rhombus + dotted basement line + tiny "SERAPHIM" wordmark. Most "esoteric/seraph" of the lineup. Could feel too occult depending on intended customer.
PRO: ritual/serious feel · CON: small text @ 32px
